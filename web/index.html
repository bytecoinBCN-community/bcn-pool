How it works

  • Fee: 0%. The network fee of each payout transaction is taken from that payout's amounts in proportion.
  • Reward split: PPLNS over the last 2× network difficulty of share work before each block.
  • Maturity: a block is credited once it is 20 blocks deep; a block that leaves the main chain earns nothing.
  • Payouts: automatic, about once an hour, for balances of at least 0.1 BCN.
  • Share difficulty adjusts to your miner to give a share about every 15 seconds. CPU miners (XMRig with "algo": "cn/0") and CryptoNight ASICs both work.
  • Fairness: every share is recomputed by the pool. Our own ASIC does not mine here; it mines solo and caps its share of the network. Blocks are sent only while an outside node confirms the pool is on the network's chain.
  • Run by the Bytecoin community, not by the Bytecoin founders. Source code.
